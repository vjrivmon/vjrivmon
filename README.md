### About Me

I'm an AI Engineer building systems that solve real problems. I run **VRLabs** (vrlabs.es), a software studio where I design and ship AI-powered products -- from RAG chatbots for companies that can't use public AI, to research tools and full-stack platforms.

I'm also a 4th-year **GTI student at UPV Gandia** and co-founder of **Zyndra**, an assistive robotics startup. National Champion of **Telefonica Lideres Digitales Universitarios 2024**.
